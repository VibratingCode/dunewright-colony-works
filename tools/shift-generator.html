Weather and heat

HI/air = NWS heat index in shade up to 124°F, air temperature from 125°F (sets the heat level). WBGT is an estimate: the outdoor value (sun by day) for outdoor jobs, the shade value for jobs under cover. Follow whichever of heat level and WBGT is more restrictive.

Jobs

Flags add their own needs: hot work = hot-work + fire watch; confined space = attendant + entrant; crane lift = crane operator + signal person + rigger; excavation and at height = competent person; energized electrical = qualified electrical worker. Every crew needs a first aider. Deck 1 and above always counts as at height.

Tags

A complication with no condition tags (medical, equipment, people only) can always come up. Otherwise at least one tag must fit the shift: heat when it is hot, wind when it is windy, lift when a job has a crane lift, and so on.
