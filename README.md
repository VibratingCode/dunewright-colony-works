# Dunewright Colony Works — Post-Stasis Refresher

Safety refresher training for a roleplay setting: a 15,000-person construction crew revived from stasis to build a city on a desert world (75–180°F).

**Live site:** https://vibratingcode.github.io/dunewright-colony-works/

## What's here

- **Modules 1–9 + Supplement A**: orientation, dust and PPE, heat, working at height, hot work, cranes and heavy equipment, lockout/tagout, advanced materials, emergencies, confined spaces
- **Reference sheets**: pocket card, radio, org chart (by station), hand signals (crane, spotter, site emergency)
- **Tools**: site weather office, heat field card
- **exports/**: a one-page PDF and a PNG of every poster

Each module ends with a sign-off. Players fill in their name and colonist ID to stamp it complete; progress is saved in their own browser.

Dunewright Colony Works is fictional. Safety content is adapted from real standards (OSHA, NIOSH, ANSI, ASME, NFPA, CDC, US Army) but this is not real-world training or medical advice.

## Regenerating the PDF/PNG exports

`site-notes/export.ps1` in the source project drives headless Microsoft Edge to render every page.
