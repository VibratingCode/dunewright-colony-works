Rev. 5 · Post-Stasis Refresher program

Covers 9 modules (HS-01 to HS-09), 4 supplements (HS-SA, HS-SB, HS-SC, HS-SD), 6 reference sheets and 2 Atmospheric Office tools. All carry Rev. 5. Rev. 4 copies are withdrawn: pull them from muster points and replace.

How it was reviewed

  1. Four independent reviews: health and heat; height, hot work and lifting; energy, chemicals, emergencies and confined spaces; program-wide consistency and gaps.
  2. Every real-world number checked against its source standard.
  3. Conflicts between pages settled in the controlled fact list, then applied to every page.
  4. Gaps closed with new pages: electrical, scaffolds and aerial lifts, night work and fatigue, permits, roles and glossary.
  5. Second-round verification of every revised page by two more independent reviewers. They found 24 remaining blockers (2 that could hurt). All 24 were fixed and every page was then cleared.

Biggest corrections

  • Siren tones now have set lengths. Leaving a shelter needs the voice all-clear on Ch 1.
  • Power cells are always live and can't be drained. Only capacitors are discharged.
  • START triage restored: "starts breathing once the airway is opened" = RED.
  • Radio lost mid-lift: the operator stops. Each lift runs on its own talkgroup.
  • Work/rest table updated to TB MED 507 (2022) with a Very heavy column. No salt tablets.
  • Desert heat limits for gas cylinders, synthetic slings and eyewash water.
  • One wind table, one check-in rule and one after-call radio rule on every page.
