Welcome back.

You've been asleep a long time. Learn the site before the site teaches you.

Revival clearance

You are not cleared to work until Med Bay says so.

Stasis leaves you dehydrated, weak and slow to react for the first days. Your balance, vision and grip strength come back gradually. You may feel fine and still fail the checks.

4Checks to pass
0Vehicles or machines until cleared
5Days on the RAMP sticker
  • Weigh-in and hydration check at Med Bay. Your baseline weight goes on your badge record for the heat program.
  • Balance and vision check. Fail either and you stay on ground-level light duty. No ladders, no height work.
  • Grip and reaction check. Required before you touch powered tools, cranes or vehicles.
  • RAMP sticker on your helmet for Days 1–5. Everyone who sees it knows to watch you.

Channel 1 rule

Every radio on site monitors Ch 1, Site Emergency, at all times. Work on your crew channel, but keep Ch 1 on scan.

  • Ch 1 is for emergencies only. No chatter, no checks.
  • Emergency call: "Emergency, emergency, emergency", then type, location, number hurt.
  • "ALL STOP, ALL STOP" on any channel stops every crane, machine and torch in earshot until cleared.
  • Full channel plan: see the radio reference (REF-RAD).

First shift

In this order · Your crew lead signs each step
  1. Pass revival clearance at Med Bay and collect your badge, radio and RAMP sticker.
  2. Draw PPE from the Quartermaster: helmet, boots, gloves, eye and hearing protection, cooling vest. Get fit-tested for your respirator (Module 2).
  3. Report to your crew lead at your sector muster point. Get your crew channel and buddy.
  4. Walk the route with your buddy: nearest Forward Aid Station, storm shelter, cooling station and muster point.
  5. Radio check on your crew channel. Confirm Ch 1 is on scan.
  6. Light duty only for your ramp share of the shift (20% on Day 1). Your crew lead logs it.
  7. Finish the refresher modules before you take on any task they cover. Modules are posted at every muster point and on the refresher index.

Chain of command

Crew of 10–12 → Crew Lead → Foreman → General Foreman → Sector Superintendent → Deputy Director → Colony Construction Director.

Go to your crew lead first. Safety concerns can go straight to any green helmet. Full chart: REF-ORG org chart.

Badge rules

  • Wear it on your chest, photo out, every shift.
  • Your badge lists every machine, permit and module you're cleared for. No badge entry, no task.
  • Never lend it. Never badge someone else through a gate.
  • Lost badge: report to Security on Ch 2 the same shift.

Report near misses

A near miss is an accident that didn't happen this time. Report every one to your crew lead or a green helmet before the end of shift.

No blame for reporting. The next crew depends on what you tell us.

Stop-work authority

Every worker on this site can stop work when something looks unsafe. No penalty, no questions. Stop it, make it safe, tell your crew lead. Refresher modules: index.

Worker acknowledgement

I have passed revival clearance, I know my sector, muster point, nearest shelter and aid station, I keep Ch 1 on scan, and I know the siren signals.
