Post-stasis refresher program

All 15,210 revived workers complete nine modules and one supplement before their first full shift. Your crew lead checks your sign-offs. Work through them in order. Module 1 tells you where everything is. Module 3 starts your heat ramp.

Each module ends with a sign-off. Fill in your name, colonist ID, crew and date to stamp it complete. Your progress saves in this browser only.
