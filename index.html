Post-stasis refresher program

All 15,210 revived workers complete nine modules and four supplements. Work through them in order. Module 1 (HS-01) tells you where everything is. Day 1 of your heat ramp is your first shift with any heat exposure after Revival Med Bay clears you (Module 3, HS-03).

Before Day 1Finish HS-01, HS-02, HS-03 and HS-09 before Day 1 at any work face.
Before 100%Finish all nine modules and all four supplements before your first 100% shift.
Before the taskThat task's module or supplement, and the badge endorsement, before you do the task. No badge entry, no task.

Each module and supplement ends with a sign-off. Fill in your name, Colonist ID, crew and date to stamp it. That stamp is your own copy and saves only in this browser. Your crew lead records your completion on your badge record at the sector office, and the badge record is the one that counts.

    Stop-work authority

    Anyone can stop unsafe work. No penalty, no questions. Stop it, make it safe, tell your crew lead (your foreman if there's no crew lead; any green hat if it isn't fixed). Work restarts only when the crew lead confirms the fix with the person who stopped it. Every stop is logged.
