Trades in every sector

  • Steel & ironwork: erection, bolting, decking
  • Structural print & concrete: printed walls, pours, finishing
  • Framing & panels: hab shells, cladding, seals
  • Site services: trenching, utilities, pipe
  • Finishing & fit-out: interiors, hatches, fixtures
  • General labor: laydown, cleanup, water runs, cooling stations

Cranes, heavy equipment, fabrication, polymers and power are site-wide departments. They're assigned into sectors job by job and take direction from the sector on site.
