Rules for every permit

Read it · post it · stop when it changes

Before work starts

  • The signer walks the job and checks the permit board before signing. Never from an office. (site rule)
  • The signer runs the conflict check below.
  • Read the permit before you start. Your name on it means you've been briefed on its hazards and controls.
  • Posted at the job and on the board, or no start.

While it's live

Something changes: stop. The permit is suspended until the signer re-checks the job. (site rule)

  • Wind 20+ mph or a blowing-dust advisory (hot work).
  • A meter alarm: 1% LEL for hot work, 10% LEL for entry, or O₂ outside 19.5–23.5%.
  • A sandstorm warning, a flood warning, a lightning alert, a take-shelter or evacuate siren, or ALL STOP. A sandstorm watch or the weekly siren test doesn't suspend permits.
  • A new task, location or crew.

Missing or expired permit at a job: use your stop-work authority.

The permit board

  • One at every structure. Work on open ground uses the board at the sector office. (site rule)
  • It holds every live permit, LP- plan and fall-rescue plan, and every group lockout with the group-lockout lead's name. (site rule)
  • Signers check it for conflicting work before they sign.
  • Closed permits come off the board and go to Safety & Health for the record. (site rule)

Conflict checks (SIMOPS)

Simultaneous operations
Checked before the second permit is signed

SIMOPS means two jobs close enough to endanger each other. Before signing, the signer checks the permit board and walks the area. The second job isn't signed until both signers agree how the jobs stay apart, and write it on both permits. (site rule)

Hot work near spraying

VeilCoat solvent and spray mist burn. No hot work within 35 ft of VeilCoat or any other solvent spraying (PTW-SP), while it runs or until the solvent flashes off.

Do: schedule them apart, or put 35 ft and a barrier between them. Test the air below 1% LEL before the torch lights.

Loads over hot work or entries

No load path over a hot-work zone, an open hatch, an attendant or a trench crew. Slag and sparks cut and melt synthetic slings.

Do: the Lift Director reroutes the lift, or the other job pauses and clears out while the load passes.

Inert-gas purges near entries

Argon and nitrogen vent from purged tanks and hull voids and pool in low spots: pits, trenches, the next hatch over.

Do: route purge vents away from hatches, trenches and air intakes. The entry supervisor checks the board for purges before signing a PTW-CS, and the air is tested at the hatch.

Shared lockouts

Two crews on one machine or circuit share one group lockout: yellow job locks, keys in the yellow lockbox, every worker's red lock on the box, one group-lockout lead. Crews under different foremen: the foremen agree on one lead and write the name on the lockbox tag. If they can't agree, the general foreman names the lead.

Do: Electrical & Power's blue locks stay on until their own release. Nothing is re-energized until every crew's locks are off (HS-07).

Also check

  • Digging near a crane: no excavation beside or under outrigger mats unless the Lift Director and the excavation competent person agree.
  • Engines and exhaust: keep generators, vehicles and compressors away from confined-space air intakes (HS-SA).
  • Energized work: barricade the limited approach boundary of a PTW-EE job. No other crew works inside it (HS-SB).
  • Work below work: no crew under overhead work, hot work from above, or a load path.

When signers disagree

  • Foremen who can't agree take it to their general foreman, then the Assistant Super on shift. (site rule)
  • Until it's settled, the second job waits.
  • Any green hat can stop both jobs.

Stop-work authority

No permit, an expired permit, or a conflict nobody checked: that's unsafe work. Anyone can stop unsafe work. No penalty, no questions. Stop it, make it safe, tell your crew lead (your foreman if there's no crew lead; any green hat if it isn't fixed). Work restarts only when the crew lead confirms the fix with the person who stopped it. Every stop is logged.
