How to carry it

  • Print it, or use the PDF, and fold it into four along the dashed lines with Panel 1 on the outside.
  • Keep it in a chest pocket, not your pants. Responders check the chest.
  • Update your sector, crew and muster point every time you're reassigned.
  • Lost card? The Quartermaster issues a new one on Ch 15.
