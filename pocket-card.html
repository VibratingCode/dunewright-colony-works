RAMP band (black-and-yellow, reads RAMP) on your helmet from Day 1. Off only when your crew lead logs Day 5.

Heat stroke

Collapsed or not fully alert on a hot site = heat stroke until a medic says otherwise. Ch 1 "Heat casualty". Cool first (ice trough to the neck), transport second. No drinks if confused.

Heat exhaustion: pull off, cool, sips of water. Someone walks them to the Forward Aid Station (FAS) now, never alone.

Types: Heat casualty · Man down · Fall · Fire · Gas · Chemical · Structural · Struck-by · Caught-in · Electrical · Confined space · Flood · Sandstorm.

ALL STOP

"ALL STOP, ALL STOP" on any channel, or 3 short blasts: stop all motion, hot work and machines. Hold position. A suspended load lands only on the signal person's direction. Cleared on the same channel by whoever called it, or by command.

Never leave a shelter or restart work on a tone alone. Wait for the Ch 1 voice all clear or your crew lead. Siren test: every 7th day at 1200, announced on Ch 1.

Warnings · Atmos on Ch 16, repeated on Ch 1
WatchSandstorm possible within 2 h: secure loose material, plan how loads come down, stage near shelter.
WarningSandstorm within 30 min: land all loads, booms down, climbers and MEWPs down, go to shelter.
FloodCalled by grid: leave wadis, trenches and low cuts at once.
LightningStrikes within 10 mi: booms down, off steel, out of MEWPs, into a sealed shelter or closed vehicle, never under steel. Resume 30 min after the last strike.

Bleeding: press hard. Tourniquet 2–3 in above the wound, not on a joint. Write the time. Every crew carries a trauma kit with two tourniquets.

CPR: push hard and fast in the center of the chest, 100–120 a minute, 2–2.4 in deep. Let the chest come all the way back up. Get the AED (automated external defibrillator).

Scan rule: every radio scans Ch 1 + Ch 16 + your sector channel. Crane, equipment and hot-work crews add their trade channel. Hub Command repeats every siren and every sandstorm, flood and lightning warning on Ch 1.

Crew channel = your foreman talkgroup (e.g. "C-F14"). Each lift runs on its own lift talkgroup.

Location: Sector-grid, structure, deck, landmark. "Charlie-14, Tower B, Deck 3, east stair." Say numbers digit by digit: "one-four", "niner".

Check-ins: buddies watch each other all shift. Your crew lead sees you at least every 30 min and checks in with the foreman every 30 min. Missed check-in: call-back at once. No answer in 5 min: someone goes in person.

Lone work: none at Caution heat or higher, at height, in confined spaces, or near wadis or trenches. Otherwise 15-min check-ins and a logged location.

Fighting a fire: only if it's waste-bin size, your exit is behind you and you have the right extinguisher. Not out in about 10 seconds: leave and call Ch 1 "Fire". Never fight a polymer, power-cell or gas-fed fire. Polymer fire: bulk feedstock, a curing resin mass, a printed structure or panel stack, or a spray zone. Rags or scrap in a waste-bin-size fire may be fought; smother a rag can with its lid. A fire that's out: tell your foreman, who logs it on Ch 13.

Stop-work authority: anyone can stop unsafe work. No penalty, no questions. Stop it, make it safe, tell your crew lead (your foreman if there's no crew lead; any green hat if it isn't fixed). Work restarts only when the crew lead confirms the fix with the person who stopped it. Every stop is logged.

Lockout: one person, one lock, one key. Your red personal lock carries your Colonist ID.

Report it: every injury, illness, first aid case, near miss, damage and unsafe condition, to your crew lead before the end of the shift (at once if serious), on form HS-IR. No retaliation.

Your entries save in this browser only. Fill in name and Colonist ID once and every module's sign-off picks them up. This card is your own copy. Your badge record is the one that counts.

How to carry it

  • Print it, or use the PDF, and fold it into four along the dashed lines with Panel 1 on the outside.
  • Keep it in a chest pocket, not your pants. Responders check the chest.
  • Update your sector, crew, talkgroup and muster point every time you're reassigned.
  • Lost card? The Quartermaster issues a new one on Ch 15.
